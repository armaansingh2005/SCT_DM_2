Voice Guide

✅ Do's

Use Friendly Language

"Grab your favorite brew and start your day with positive energy."

Encourage Community

"Join thousands of coffee lovers sharing their morning rituals."

Stay Authentic

"We source our beans responsibly because quality matters."

❌ Don'ts

Don't Sound Robotic

"Our beverage solutions optimize daily consumption efficiency."

Don't Use Negative Messaging

"If you don't buy this coffee, you're missing out."

Don't Overuse Jargon

"Utilize our premium caffeine distribution ecosystem."
